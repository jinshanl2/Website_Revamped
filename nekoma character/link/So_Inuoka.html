SŌ INUOKA

About Inuoka

Sō Inuoka (Japanese: 犬岡 走 Inuoka Sō) was a first-year student at Nekoma High. He was initially one of the middle blockers but was later switched to a wing spiker for the Boys' Volleyball Team.
