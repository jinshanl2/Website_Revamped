TSUTOMU GOSHIKI

About Goshiki

Tsutomu Goshiki (Japanese: 五色 工 Goshiki Tsutomu) is a first-year student at Shiratorizawa Academy. He is the only first-year to play as a starting regular on the volleyball team as a wing spiker and is acknowledged as the team's up-and-coming ace.
